Paper: "The Second Opinion: SOX 404 Before, Now and After" (Uniqus, Oct 2026). Checked 2026-10-08.

## Access limits (read first)

- **SEC docket.** sec.gov is blocked by the egress proxy (curl gets a 403 on CONNECT). WebFetch cannot resolve DNS. Through TinyFish:
  - The new SEC index (`/rules-regulations/public-comments/s7-2026-18`) loads only **page 1** (30 most recent rows). `?page=N` is ignored.
  - The legacy index URL `s7202618.htm` returns 404.
  - **Every SEC-hosted PDF letter returns `target_unreachable`.** HTM letters open (for example, Olema).
  - TinyFish browser automation, which could have paged the index, failed because the wallet balance is $0.
- **Primary text came from the firms' own copies of their SEC letters:**
  - PwC: Viewpoint PDF
  - EY: ey.com PDF
  - Grant Thornton (GT): gt.com PDF
  - BDO: arch.bdo.com PDF
  - RSM: rsmus.com PDF
  - CBIZ: the firm's own summary of its letter, not the letter itself
- **No primary text could be opened for** Deloitte (the DART page renders an empty PDF viewer), KPMG, Crowe, CohnReznick, Baker Tilly or Connor Group. Their SEC URLs appear in search results but cannot be fetched.
- **Secondary sources used as cross-checks:**
  - Nicholas Hallman's record-by-record analysis of the docket, nicholashallman.com/comment-letter-analysis/egc-accommodations-2026/. It is a snapshot taken 2026-09-07 with 188 records, each with a summary and a stance label.
  - Goodwin alert (Oct 2026), goodwinlaw.com/…/alerts-practices-pcap-secs-filer-status-proposal-key-themes-emerging.
  - Verdicts that rest on these sources say so.
- **The tracker** (tzachizach.github.io/sec-filer-status-tracker) opened. The header says "Last updated 2026-10-01", but its data is rendered by JavaScript and did not load. Only search-engine snippets of its content were available:
  - "As of 2026-09-04, the docket holds 173 comment letters (164 engage at least one element)".
  - "B2 — removing the §404(b) auditor attestation — … ChatGPT 27 support / 57 oppose / 31 conditional" (115 engaged).
  - The paper reports 118 addressed: 32 support, 75 too broad or do not expand, 11 no position. The taxonomies differ, so the two are not directly comparable. The tracker is not authoritative.
- **No "internal working notes" were found** in the scratchpad (src/, out/ and work/ were checked).

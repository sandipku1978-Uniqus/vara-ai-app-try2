## Summary
- **Exhibit 7.** All World Bank figures and the 46% arithmetic are verified. All Renaissance figures are verified, with three nuances:
  - **2023:** the chart shows 109 / $19.5bn, while the review's table shows 108 / $19.4bn.
  - **1Q 2026:** first reported as 35 IPOs, since restated to 34.
  - **Unit offerings:** the paper correctly says 2026 data *exclude* them. The brief said "including".
- **SpaceX and SK hynix:** dates, amounts and structure are verified.
  - SpaceX priced Jun 11, 2026: $135 a share, 555.56M shares, Nasdaq.
  - SK hynix priced Jul 9, 2026: $149 per ADR, 10 ADRs per share, Nasdaq, primary listing stays in Seoul.
- **Section 6.**
  - The 2025 update is Doidge, Karolyi, Shen & Stulz (NBER w33556; Financial Review 2025). It restates the 1999 dating from the 2017 study.
  - The FPI sentences need the EGC carve-out and "worldwide float". The release defers FPI relief to the June 2025 concept release.
- **Cross-border box.**
  - The India exemption is wider than "certain small private companies": the notification says "or", not "and".
  - The ADAA claim is verified.
  - The UAE listed-company claim has a primary source: the regulator's 2025 AGM circular.
  - Saudi Arabia: Arts. 21(2)(d), 87(10) and 88(a).
- **About Uniqus.** 700+ / 85+ / eleven offices and the service list are stale. The current figures (Sep 7, 2026) are 800 / 100 / 13 offices / 400+ clients, plus Valuations.
